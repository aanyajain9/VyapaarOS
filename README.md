# VyapaarOS

## Smart Business Management System

VyapaarOS is a web-based business management system designed for small shops and businesses. It provides a centralized platform to manage inventory, sales, customers, Udhaar (credit), expenses, reports, and shop information.

The goal of VyapaarOS is to simplify everyday business operations and reduce dependence on manual records by bringing important business activities into one system.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Small businesses often manage products, sales, customer information, credit transactions, and expenses using notebooks or separate systems. This can make it difficult to track stock, monitor pending Udhaar, understand business performance, and maintain accurate records.

VyapaarOS provides a centralized business management system where these activities can be managed from a single interface.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objectives of VyapaarOS are:

- Centralize daily business operations
- Manage products and inventory
- Record and manage sales
- Maintain customer information
- Track Udhaar and customer payments
- Record business expenses
- Provide useful business reports
- Display important business information through a dashboard
- Provide a responsive interface for desktop and mobile devices

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📊 Dashboard

The dashboard provides an overview of the business, including:

- Today's sales
- Today's profit
- Pending Udhaar
- Low-stock items
- Today's expenses
- Recent sales
- Low-stock alerts
- Quick actions for common operations

### 📦 Inventory Management

- Add new products
- Edit products
- Delete products
- Search products
- Manage stock quantity
- Track purchase and selling prices
- Identify low-stock products

### 🛒 Sales Management

- Select customer
- Select product
- Enter quantity
- Select payment method
- Complete sales
- Update inventory after sales
- Maintain sales records

### 👥 Customer Management

- Add customers
- Edit customer information
- Delete customers
- Search customers
- Maintain customer details

### 💰 Udhaar / Credit Management

- Add customer Udhaar
- Receive customer payments
- Track pending Udhaar
- View customer balances
- Maintain credit transaction history

### 💸 Expense Management

- Add business expenses
- Record expense category
- Record amount and date
- Track business expenses

### 📈 Reports

The reports section provides an overview of:

- Total sales
- Gross profit
- Total expenses
- Net profit
- Pending Udhaar
- Low-stock items
- Recent sales

### 👤 Profile

The profile section allows the shop/business owner to manage:

- Shop name
- Owner information
- Phone number
- Email
- Address

### 📱 Responsive Design

VyapaarOS provides:

- Desktop sidebar navigation
- Mobile-friendly layout
- Mobile bottom navigation
- Responsive forms and dashboard cards
